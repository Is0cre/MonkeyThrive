Clinical safety

Plain limits for a tool that stays on your device.

What this is

MonkeyThrive is a supportive self-regulation tool. It does not replace professional therapy. It does not diagnose, assess, or treat any condition.

Window of Tolerance

A check-in asks how you are right now. Higher-intensity tools stay closed when that check-in is outside a regulated state. The gates are fixed rules in the app. They are not a score, and they are not a diagnosis.

Bilateral cueing is for grounding and attention. It is not EMDR and it is not trauma reprocessing. Wim Hof-style breathing asks for safety confirmations at the start of each session.

What the app will not do

There is no account, no streak, and no engagement ranking. The app does not send journal entries, check-ins, or sensor readings anywhere. The country you pick in the crisis directory stays in local storage.

Need immediate human support? MonkeyThrive includes an offline crisis directory, or reach out to local emergency services directly.

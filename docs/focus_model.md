# Focus Model

Focus levels are structured subjective attention states. They are labels for user experience design, not claims about objective external realities.

No focus level is diagnostic. No focus level proves anything metaphysical. No focus level should be used to pressure the user into belief, disclosure, or continued use.

## Focus 1

Normal waking baseline.

Use:

- Orientation.
- Pre-check.
- Post-check.
- Ordinary reflection.

## Focus 10

Mind awake, body deeply relaxed.

Use:

- Relaxation.
- Body scan.
- Grounding.
- Sleep preparation.

## Focus 11

Receptive learning or suggestion channel.

Use:

- User-authored affirmations only.
- No hidden persuasion.
- No system-authored identity claims.
- No clinical claims.

If the user did not write or explicitly approve the suggestion, do not present it in this state.

## Focus 12

Expanded awareness.

Use:

- Creativity.
- Reflection.
- Problem solving.
- Journaling.

Do not claim enhanced perception or external truth.

## Focus 15

Altered time perception.

Use:

- Long-view reflection.
- Perspective.
- Life timeline reflection where clinically safe.

Never claim actual time travel.

## Focus 18

Compassion, gratitude, and acceptance.

Use:

- Loving-kindness style framing.
- Self-compassion.
- Gratitude reflection.
- Acceptance practice.

Avoid pressure to forgive, reconcile, disclose, or reinterpret trauma.

## Focus 21

Advanced meditative bridge state.

Use:

- Symbolic exploration only.
- Belief-neutral framing.
- Strong return and grounding.

No objective claims.

## Focus 22-27

Optional advanced symbolic or non-ordinary experience space.

Use:

- User belief-neutral exploration.
- Symbolic journaling.
- Meaning-making chosen by the user.

Do not claim:

- Afterlife access.
- Spirits.
- Remote viewing.
- Objective nonphysical locations.
- Verified external information.

## Progression

Focus progression must be conservative. Progression changes require clinical review. Users must be able to stay at lower focus levels indefinitely. No streaks, rewards, or scarcity mechanics may push advancement.

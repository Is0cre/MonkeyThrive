# Clinical Governance

Clinical safety is not delegated to the LLM. Deterministic rules control access to safety-sensitive tools and states.

## Mandatory Clinical Review

Clinical review is mandatory before changing:

- Gateway gates.
- Wim Hof gates.
- Bilateral stimulation gates.
- Medication wording.
- Suicide or self-harm support flows.
- Trauma-processing-adjacent features.
- Session intensity changes.
- Focus-level progression.
- Return-phase removal or alteration.
- Any claim that could be read as diagnosis.
- Any notification wording that could create guilt, fear, or dependency.

## Safety Authority

The safety authority is:

1. Deterministic clinical gates.
2. User self-report.
3. Conservative defaults.
4. Human clinical review for high-risk changes.

The LLM is not a safety authority.

## User Authority

The user is the authority on their internal experience. The app may ask. It must not label.

Do not infer:

- Anxiety.
- Depression.
- Panic.
- Dissociation.
- Trauma state.
- Medication misuse.
- Suicidal intent.

If acute risk is explicitly disclosed or detected by deterministic rules, route to the documented crisis flow. Do not improvise with an LLM.

## Gateway Governance

Gateway is optional, advanced, and clinically conservative. It is not therapy, EMDR, trauma processing, diagnosis, or proof of objective external realities.

Gateway changes requiring clinical review include:

- Entry gates.
- Contraindication language.
- Session duration ranges.
- Intensity settings.
- Focus progression.
- Return phase.
- Grounding phase.
- Claims about non-ordinary experience.

The return phase is mandatory and cannot be skipped.

## Bilateral Stimulation Governance

Bilateral stimulation must not be labelled EMDR unless a properly governed EMDR protocol exists with qualified clinical oversight. Current bilateral cueing must remain framed as regulation or attention support, not trauma reprocessing.

## Wim Hof Governance

Wim Hof-style breathing is higher risk than ordinary paced breathing. Safety confirmations and Window of Tolerance gates must remain deterministic and per-session. Any relaxation of these gates requires clinical review.

## Documentation Requirement

Every safety-sensitive feature must have:

- A plain-language purpose.
- Contraindications.
- Deterministic gates.
- Stop conditions.
- User control points.
- Storage implications.
- Clinical review owner or status.

What it is

Practices for the nervous system, kept on this device.

MonkeyThrive is a small set of tools: paced breathing, a physiological sigh, cold water, sensory grounding, vagal cues, and bilateral stimulation. It runs in the browser and as a standalone Android app. The release build is made to work with no network permission.

You start with a body map. Head, throat, chest, and stomach can be marked tight or heavy. The app shows that reading as overwhelmed, present, or flat or numb, and you see it before you continue. An empty map does not count as present. You are the authority on your own experience. The reading is not a diagnosis.

The Monkey King stays in the name: self-made strength that learns a limit. In the app, that limit is practical. A tool that needs more capacity than the moment can hold stays closed.

Why it is different

Built to be left alone, and easy to leave.

Strictly offline

The release app has no network permission. Check-ins, journal entries, and readings stay on the device. There is no telemetry, no crash reporting, and no analytics.

Clinically gated

Window of Tolerance rules decide which tools open. The lock is the same for overwhelmed and for flat or numb. The rules are fixed. They are not a score, and they are not a diagnosis.

No guilt architecture

No streaks, no accounts, and no engagement algorithms. There is no score for the days you were away. The app is built to be easy to put down.

Open source and verifiable

The source is public under GPL-3.0. The offline claim, the gates, and the crisis directory can be read in the repository.

Who it is for

For people who want the tool, and nothing around it.

  • Trauma survivors who want regulation practice that stays on the device.
  • People who want no account, no telemetry, and no profile.
  • People on de-Googled Android, including GrapheneOS, CalyxOS, and Sailfish OS.
  • Anyone who wants a small set of practices without a wellness platform around them.

Features

Some tools wait until the reading is present.

A check-in comes first. The same higher-intensity tools stay closed when the reading is overwhelmed and when it is flat or numb. Present is the only reading that opens them.

Always available

  • Box breathing. A steady count you can follow.
  • Physiological sigh. Two inhales and a long exhale.
  • Cold water. A short timer for the diving reflex.
  • 5-4-3-2-1 grounding. Name what you can sense around you.
  • Vagal prompts. Humming, gargling, or a longer exhale.
  • Anchor. The simplest screen, on the bar. On Android, the volume keys open it. Words stay off for the first few breaths.

Gated, regulated state only

  • 4-7-8 breathing. A longer pattern. Open when the reading is present.
  • Wim Hof breathing. Open when the reading is present, after the safety confirmations for that session.
  • Safe place. A visualisation stored on this device. Open when the reading is present.
  • Bilateral stimulation. For grounding and attention. It is not EMDR. It also waits until earlier parts of the app have been used, and it still requires a present reading.

Safety and support

A tool can sit with you. A person can help.

If you need support now

The app includes an offline crisis directory, kept on the device and listed by country. You can also contact local emergency services directly. A call or text from that directory uses your phone’s own dialer or messages app.

MonkeyThrive is a supportive self-regulation tool. It does not replace professional therapy, and it does not diagnose, assess, or treat any condition.
